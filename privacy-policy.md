# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-12-29

Thank you for using **Waddle Huddle** (“the App”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information We Collect

**Waddle Huddle does not collect, store, use, or share any personal information.**

The App does not:
- Collect or store personal identifiers (such as name, email address, or phone number)
- Collect or store precise or approximate location data
- Collect usage analytics, diagnostics, or advertising data
- Use cookies or tracking technologies

## Bluetooth and Location Access

Waddle Huddle uses **Bluetooth** and may request **location permission** solely to enable core app functionality, such as discovering and communicating with nearby devices.

- Bluetooth and location access are used **in real time only**
- No Bluetooth data or location data is collected, stored, logged, or transmitted
- All processing happens locally on your device
- Data is discarded immediately after use
